# Design references

The CAD here is original geometry. Reference models and supplier photographs
were not scaled or copied into the stand, wheel or outlet CAD.

- [Bambu filament drying and spool reference](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/filament-acc/filament/dry-filament): the study uses the published 200 mm diameter × 67 mm spool envelope. The 55 mm hub remains a fit reference requiring verification.
- [High-performance spool desiccant container](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1193993-high-performance-spool-desiccant-container-holder): inspiration for the visual core concept's open longitudinal air passages. The core is not yet fabrication CAD.
- [Trelleborg O-ring catalogue](https://www.trelleborg.com/seals/-/media/tss-media-repository/tss_website/pdf-and-other-literature/catalogs/o_ring_gb_en.pdf): nominal gland design reference. Printed fit, porosity and sealing are unqualified.

Downloaded third-party print models, supplier gallery images and private order
records are not included. If a future project incorporates third-party files,
retain their applicable license and provenance separately.
