# Prototype components

Purchased and container items for one drybox. The printed parts, their sources
and latest print states are in [`cad/drybox.json`](cad/drybox.json) and the
[README](README.md#current-parts). This is not a released assembly
specification.

| Component | Quantity | Current selection / interface |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Gasketed food-storage container | 1 | Actual usable interior, taper and wall thickness must be measured. The CAD study uses synthetic dimensions. |
| 608ZZ bearing | 2 | YAMASO or equivalent, 8 mm bore × 22 mm OD × 7 mm width. One bearing per lift-out carrier. |
| Pass-through push-fit fitting | 1 | [HANGLIFE pack sold as PC4-M10](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQ7W6CGR), for 4 mm OD tube. Its packaging drawing specifies a PT 1/8 thread, 7 mm long, 18 mm overall; "M10" is not used as a thread specification. The outlet's printed socket is R 1/8-compatible (`outlet.json` `purchased_interfaces`). Verify the received thread and full tube passage at the first fit. |
| PTFE printer lead | As routed | 4 mm OD × 2.5 mm ID; [selected example](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F5HG7659). One continuous removable lead; the final length depends on the printer route. |
| Crank wall O-ring | 1 | 22 mm ID × 2 mm cross-section, on the wall-facing side of the stationary fitting. |
| Crank cap O-ring | 1 | 20 mm ID × 2 mm cross-section, on the cap-facing side of the stationary fitting. |
| Outlet wall O-ring | 1 | NBR, 22 mm ID × 2 mm cross-section, in the face groove under the outside flange. |
| Outlet cap O-ring | 1 | NBR, 18 mm ID × 2 mm cross-section, on the neck beneath the storage cap. |
| Humidity monitor | Optional | The purchased [TempPro / ThermoPro TP49](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07WCR5Y4B) could not be fitted in the assembled drybox. A mount or replacement remains open. |
| Refillable desiccant | As tested | Orange indicating silica gel; [selected example](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01I5Y2DG6). Verify installed mass and bead retention through the 2.0 mm CAD vent slots. |
| Desiccant boxes and coin-slot caps | 2 pairs | Removable printed parts with one shared screw-in funnel. See the [desiccant attachment](README.md#desiccant-attachment). |


The current through-wall crank uses the same removable crank across copies.
See [fit checks](README.md#physical-fit-checks) for the printed interfaces
and loaded-spool checks.
