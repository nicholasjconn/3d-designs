Shorter cap, same sealing interfaces

Heights use the same drawing scale. The cap mouth, retention threads and both seal glands are unchanged. Space is saved by using a 20 mm tail and 3 mm nominal crown margin; no seal was removed.

Seal and service boundary

The wall ring seals the body flange to the container. The cap ring seals against a smooth cap bore above the cap-retaining thread and below all fitting/collet openings. The filament and clamp stay entirely inside this boundary. The cap encloses a straight 20 mm tail beyond the reference release face, with 3 mm nominal crown clearance. The entire PTFE lead is outside the body in storage. The actual fitting projection and the tail-length mark must be checked before this tight crown clearance is qualified.

18 × 2 mm ring glands, clearances and thread fits are a candidate geometry, subject to the actual rings, printed surfaces and container. Ring display deformation is illustrative. Neither the printed shell nor either seal has been qualified airtight. The original one-piece clamp appearance is retained; its hinge animation is illustrative and the nominal 1.75 mm jaw gap provides contact, not demonstrated squeeze or grip.

Material choice: PLA

Founder selected PLA for the printed outlet body, nut, cap and one-piece clip. Design the clip around PLA; any proposed material change needs discussion supported by a specific reason. No PETG is selected.

The comparisons below explain alternatives; they do not establish grip or fatigue life. The current clamp visual does not contain the proposed longer hinge or preloaded spring leaf; its 1.75 mm pad gap only establishes nominal contact.
