Solid jaw. Blunt hook.

The short gripping neck is gone. A broad, solid jaw joins directly to the hinge frame. Only the long outside latch is intended to flex.

What each remaining feature does

Rounded fixed jaw

Supports the filament and is too wide to enter the outlet. The filament drops into it from the side.

Captured printed hinge

Keeps the two bodies together while the upper jaw opens. No separate pin or hardware.

Solid gripping jaw

Material fills the former hinge-side gap. There is no small spring neck under the pad. The jaw is 5 mm deep, with at least 1.3 mm of material above the groove.

Flexible click latch

The sharp triangular tip is gone. The hook has a rounded, blunt nose and a full 1 mm of backing beneath its catching face. The long outside arm supplies the spring action; the lip releases it.

What we removed

The spool-mount channel, tall finger flange and branding. The outline and dimensions now come from the outlet, cap, filament and finger access.

This is independently authored parametric CAD using common hinge and latch mechanisms. It does not load or trim the downloaded mesh.

Current stage

Design prototype, not print ready. Actual grip, latch engagement, printed hinge clearance and PLA fatigue remain unqualified. The empty closed view does not simulate latch bending around filament.

Grok agrees this blunt-hook revision is ready for design review. The empty closed clip keeps 0.95 mm nominal cap margin. A simplified latch model estimates about 1% bending strain at nominal fit, reaching about 2% in sampled hinge-play positions. That is not a strength or life qualification.
