Collar: one printed part

Threaded center → three arms and struts → adjustable ring. The entire collar screws onto the spindle. Its locking key engages a spindle slot to prevent unscrewing in either rotation direction.

With a spool installed, the flat rings stop on its hub end faces while short pilots enter the center hole. Gentle collar tightening clamps the spool between them. Friction is intended to make the spool, spindle and collar turn together; the bearings support that rotating assembly.
