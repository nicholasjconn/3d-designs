The green rings are attached to two printed parts

The drive-side ring is part of the spindle. Three arms and three short struts connect it to the shaft. The other ring is part of the collar, which screws onto the spindle.

Spindle: one printed part

Shaft → three radial arms → three axial struts → fixed ring. All of these are fused into one solid. There is no ring-to-shaft assembly joint on this side.

Collar: one printed part

Threaded center → three arms and struts → adjustable ring. The entire collar screws onto the spindle. Its locking key engages a spindle slot to prevent unscrewing in either rotation direction.

With a spool installed, the tapered rings seat in the ends of its center hole. Gentle collar tightening clamps the spool between them. Friction is intended to make the spool, spindle and collar turn together; the bearings support that rotating assembly.

Actual spool grip, printed thread fit and lock operation still need the first physical fit check. The separated view illustrates part ownership; it does not simulate unscrewing the collar. These meshes come from the current native CAD build.
