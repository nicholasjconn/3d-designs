Frame and carriers

The frame has two legs per side, two upper spokes with R6 junction rounds, a central brace and three 45° rim undersides. Each frame plate has a 24.4 mm keyed slot down to a flat floor 13.2 mm below the axle; its thinned edges run in the carrier grooves. The carriers hold the 608s and lift out with the spindle; they seat by gravity only.

Outside drive: fixed X and bearing-supported wheel

The handwheel no longer turns against the container. Selected 17 November 2026; the previous wheel is preserved in commit 7f54693. All positions below assume a nominal 2 mm drive wall. The container is unmeasured.

  • Fixed X (one printed part). A hub, four diagonal arms 3 mm thick under the wheel, and four 4 mm cups centred at R77.5, outside the wheel's R60 sweep (cup edge R67.5). It also has an integral Ø11 shoulder and a Ø7.9 journal for the extra 608. Each cup holds one Ø15 × 3 magnet glued into a pocket that opens toward the wall.
  • Gray frame anchor branches. Four branches are part of the frame, ending in glue pockets with the same centres. Their faces are on the wall-contact plane, so each anchor pair is separated only by the wall. The upper branches stay out of the disc's straight-up lift path (|Z| < 40.5 above the axle), and the lower roots keep 2.0 mm from the turning disc. The frame now reaches Y168.8 (was 128). Whether that fits the container is unknown.
  • Wheel on the extra 608. The wheel's back face is 1 mm in front of the X. Its 608 pocket opens toward the wall, and an integral 1 mm lip (inner Ø19.6) bears on the outer ring, carrying the magnets' inward pull. A printed split retainer ring sits in a hub groove on the wall side of the 608. It holds the outer ring when the knob is pulled outward.
  • Inner ring. It sits against the X's Ø11 shoulder on the wall side. On the outboard side, a printed radial axle clip with an Ø11 outer edge bears on it. It works like the disc's clip, and it enters the journal groove through one notch in the wheel's lip. Every retaining face touches only its own ring: Ø11 lies within SKF's 608 shaft-abutment range (SKF 608-2Z data sheet) (10–12, inner land 12.15), and Ø19.6 within the housing range (outer land 19.2, Da max 20). The purchased 608's own lands are not measured. Axial float is 0.3 mm for each ring, so there is no preload.
  • Locked endpoints kept. The holder is 82.2 mm wall to wall, the spindle 79.4 mm, and the full assembly 104.65 mm (X −37.5…67.15); the base stays 75 × 130 mm. The wheel starts 4 mm farther out and its knob is 4 mm shorter (exposed grip 9.45 mm, was 13.45), so the knob tip stays at X67.15.
  • Drive-magnet gap. 10.1 mm face to face at a 2 mm wall (6.1 mm before the X). Coupling torque at this gap is not evaluated.

Assembly.

  1. Glue one magnet into each X pocket and each frame pocket, faces flush. Every frame magnet must attract its X magnet through the wall. Pick a polarity, mark the exposed face of each frame magnet and each X magnet so paired faces attract, and check every pair before the glue sets.
  2. Slide the 608 into the wheel's Ø22.4 hub pocket (0.2 mm radial clearance) from the wall side, against the lip. Squeeze the retainer ring into its groove behind it.
  3. Slide the wheel onto the X's journal until the inner ring meets the shoulder. Turn the wheel so its lip notch faces the clip, then push the axle clip into the journal groove.
  4. Hold the X against the outside of the wall, lined up with the frame branches. Slide the X off before lifting the gray frame out of the container, because the anchor magnets hold the frame to the wall. The spindle unit still lifts out on its own.

Service. Turn the notch to the clip, pull the clip out radially and slide the wheel off. Pry one end of the retainer ring to release it, then push the 608 out toward the wall side.

Spindle, collar and square drive

See how the green rings attach. Each ring is integral with its printed part: one with the spindle, one with the threaded collar. The collar lock holds in both rotation directions. The round 608 journals remain separate from the square drive section beyond the right bearing.

The revised seats use broad hub-face stops and short bore pilots. They replace the earlier bevel-dependent cones, which were based on a printable spool with flush hub faces. Your measured spool is 67.8 mm across the flanges and 59.6 mm between the recessed hub faces. The bore and bevel dimensions are unknown; radial pilot fit and grip must be checked on the actual spool.

The square key has four deep flats and rounded corners within the Ø8 bearing passage. Its matching blue socket must be reprinted with the spindle. The clip has a narrower mouth, and the groove remains round so its snap does not depend on the key angle. Turn the disc's clip channel upward for access, or work on the lifted-out assembly.

Spool change: lift out the assembly, pull off the left carrier and slip-fit left 608, lift the collar's locking paddle and unscrew the collar. Slide the spool off toward that end. Hold the spool near the collar while tightening so you do not twist the spool's two halves. Tighten gently and release the paddle into a lock position; clamp force and torque capacity remain unqualified.
