The empty rear recess shrinks from 1.7 mm to 0.9 mm. That remainder is open space, not another plastic wall. Removing it alone would not narrow the holder further: the central clip connection and fixed support set the disc position.

How the pieces connect

  1. Slide the two 608 bearings onto the green axle ends.
  2. Slide the blue disc onto the keyed right end. Insert the printed clip into the shaft groove through the open channel in the disc.
  3. Lower the axle and bearings into the frame’s two open bearing seats. The seat arms retain the bearing outer rings; the green axle turns inside them.

For clip or disc removal, lift the complete axle assembly out of the frame first. The disc stays separate so the right bearing can slide onto the axle. The clip retains it axially; the key carries drive torque. The green shoulders include a hollow relief to clear the shaft fillets while supporting the bearing inner rings, without loose spacers. The old frame screws, bearing caps and loose spacers are removed.

Native fit checks passed; an empty first-fit plate is prepared. Bearing fit, printed clip retention, seat flexibility, creep and physical spool fit need qualification.

The Ø8 mm journals and Ø55 mm spool grip are line-to-line references, not qualified fits. Bearing-shield clearance and the clip’s 0.45 mm radial catch at the flats still need checking.

Purchased tubes, tube inserts and O-rings remain required. The three core air passages and magnet stations remain. Outlet and tube connections are outside this redesign. The spool reference remains the unmeasured Ø200 × 67 mm envelope with Ø55 mm hub.
