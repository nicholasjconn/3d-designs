Same hardware in every study

Fixed stand

98 × 110 × 8 mm open base, 72 mm opening and 13 mm rails; axle height 104 mm. No shrinking base, telescoping parts or container adapters. Continuous A-frame profiles have rounded triangular windows and broad roots; no redundant central stems. Integral round wall stops react near axle height and remain outside the rotor sweep.

Rotating core

Ø46 × 60 mm vented cartridge, three hollow mesh passages and independent refill cap. Bambu spool proxy stays Ø200 × 67 mm. Two refill screws remain unchanged.

Two bearings

22 mm OD × 7 mm wide, 8 mm bore envelope. Inner rings turn; outer rings are captured. Open seat fit, capture, supported bearing axis, hub grip and printed strength remain unqualified.

Three magnet pairs

Three Ø15 × 3 mm magnets per rotor at R30 (60 mm pitch diameter) and 120° spacing. Same facing pole within each rotor, opposing across the wall. Front-seated magnets in rear-open glued pockets; no screw covers.

One coherent stand: the base, two continuous A-frames, open bearing cradles and firm drive-side wall stops form one native CAD body. Rounded triangular windows leave continuous load paths into the base. No opposite-wall contact or whole-arm flexure is selected. Native connectivity does not qualify strength, stiffness, bearing capture or physical stability; the footprint is unchanged. Stand material remains unselected; PLA selection applies to the outlet and clip only.

Magnet pockets: both rotors hold three Ø15 × 3 mm magnets at R30 (60 mm pitch diameter), in Ø15.2 mm blind bores with 1.3 mm closed front skins and 1.2 mm radial cup walls. The 0.1 mm nominal radial magnet clearance is illustrative, not a qualified fit or adhesive specification. The inside rotor is Ø80 mm, with an R39–40 rim; the old Ø58 mm disk cannot contain this layout and is no longer offered.

Outside wheel: Ø120 mm with three spokes, a 3.5 mm inward rim and an R54 knob. The rim and spokes sit 1.3 mm behind the cup fronts and are 3 mm deep. Knob-cylinder length stays 17.15 mm. Manual leverage remains set by the R54 knob; changing magnet pitch changes the magnetic coupling and requires the separate torque comparison. The inside air gap and central pad are each now 1.5 mm; the stand’s round wall stops move outside the Ø80 mm rotor. The native outside wheel remains 9.322 cm³: moving the cups along the same spokes does not change its solid volume. The ideal coupling model predicts about 22% more peak torque than the previous R20 layout at the default assumed wall, including the extra 1 mm pole-face gap; same magnet strength, friction excluded. This prediction is not physical qualification. Spoke, cup, adhesive and knob-root strength, support needs and physical performance remain unqualified.

The central 1.5 mm pad is intentional compliant wall contact. Repulsion after phase slip, handling, adhesive retention, contact drag and wall wear need tests. CAD solid volume excludes supports, purge and other print overhead.

Air path: box air → open grip sectors → three empty longitudinal passages → mesh walls → desiccant. There is no fixed inner axle or carrier. Load path: rotating core → bearings → fixed stand → wall stops. Actual wall flex, stand strength, sustained clamp grip and torque are unqualified.

Nominal range checked

The prior R30 common-envelope model cleared all 32 endpoint combinations of W 106–160 mm, H 206–280 mm, D 204–300 mm, F 0–3 mm per side and assumed wall thickness 1–4 mm in this ideal model. The new native stand preserves that footprint and placement; its affected fit and bearing-service checks were performed separately. Nominal inside and outside rotor gaps at the default taper are only 0.349 / 0.381 mm; this is a clearance-limited study range, not a qualified container compatibility list. Wider boxes add space on the left; they do not improve the drive-wall gaps.

Grok independently reviewed the complete native stand and its fit/service checks, agreeing it is ready for a limited concept handoff. Its earlier R30 layout, envelope and torque review remains applicable to the unchanged drive. The prior envelope metrics matched the independent calculation across all 32 cases; this is reused envelope evidence, not a new full-range native-stand test. Real floor corners, lid/opening, fore/aft taper, outlet and monitor space, pad contact, loads and physical performance remain unqualified.
